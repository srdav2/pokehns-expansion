# Gilded boss design notes (pass 2)

Tiers and fairness rules: `docs/GILDED_PASS_2.md` §1. Sets live in `src/data/trainers_hns.party`. Hard-cap check assumes a Hard-cap player is 2–3 levels under the listed anchor with a counter type and the party-wide Exp. Share.

## Chad Grindwell 1 — Slowpoke Well (TRAINER_CHAD_GRINDWELL_SLOWPOKE_WELL_HNS)
Levels: 14–16. Tier T1.
Gimmick: "grindset" — Guts Machop, Intimidate Tauros ace.
Threat: Bulk Up Machop snowballs if ignored; Tauros Pursuit punishes switching a Ghost/Psychic out.
Intended counterplay: Flying (Pidgey line), Psychic, Gastly.
Hard-cap check: winnable at Lv 13–15 with a Flying type and two potions.

## Colonel Buckshot 1 — Ball Show (TRAINER_COLONEL_BUCKSHOT_BALL_SHOW_HNS)
Levels: 21–23. Tier T1 (optional fight).
Gimmick: "every Pokémon is a cannon"; Sniper + Scope Lens crit fishing.
Threat: Clauncher Swords Dance plus Mega Launcher Water Pulse.
Counterplay: Grass (Bellsprout, Oddish); Electric for everything but Magnemite.
Hard-cap check: fine at Lv 20+ with a Grass or Electric type.

## Warden Pell 1 — Ecruteak gate (TRAINER_WARDEN_PELL_ECRUTEAK_GATE_HNS)
Levels: 24–26. Tier T1.
Gimmick: "pure single types"; Serene Grace + King's Rock flinch, Glare paralysis.
Threat: Stantler Hypnosis (the team's only sleep move).
Counterplay: Fighting, Ghost (Normal immunity), Water/Grass vs Sudowoodo.
Hard-cap check: fine at Lv 23+ with Gastly line or a Fighting type.

## Commissar Bleak — Goldenrod Underground, optional (TRAINER_COMMISSAR_BLEAK_GOLDENROD_HNS)
Levels: 22–23. Tier T1. Class Talk Radio Host, pic Aroma Lady.
Gimmick: podcast cameo; Hattrem Magic Bounce + Calm Mind.
Threat: Calm Mind Hattrem stalling with Leftovers.
Counterplay: Dark/Ghost/Steel; hit Hattrem physically.
Hard-cap check: optional; winnable at Lv 20+.

## Sage Purely — Olivine Lighthouse (TRAINER_SAGE_PURELY_OLIVINE_LIGHTHOUSE_HNS)
Levels: 29–31 (the bible's 34–36 band was too high; she precedes Chuck at 31). Tier T2.
Gimmick: status plus drain stall (herbal remedies). Trainer bag Super Potion / Full Heal (Energy Root / Heal Powder not used).
Threat: Weepinbell Sleep Powder (the team's one sleep move), Stun Spore x2.
Counterplay: Fire, Flying, Psychic, Steel (Magnemite walls the team).
Hard-cap check: winnable at Lv 27–29 with Fire/Flying.

## Brad Grindwell 1 — Alpha Dojo (TRAINER_BRAD_GRINDWELL_ALPHA_DOJO_HNS)
Levels: 30–32. Tier T2. Class Sigma Bro, pic Archer.
Gimmick: speed snowball (Agility) then Counter trap on Lycanroc-Midnight.
Counterplay: Water, Ground; special attackers bypass Counter.
Hard-cap check: winnable at Lv 28–30 with a Water type.

## Barry Blaster 1 — Mahogany hideout (TRAINER_BARRY_BLASTER_MAHOGANY_HNS)
Levels: 32–34. Tier T2. Only fought in `RocketHideout_B3F_hns`.
Gimmick: rage; Anger Point + Moxie snowball.
Threat: Gyarados Dragon Dance with Lum Berry.
Counterplay: Electric, Rock, Fairy.
Hard-cap check: winnable at Lv 30–32 with an Electric type.

## Barry Blaster 2 — Radio Tower 5F fake Director (TRAINER_BARRY_BLASTER_RADIO_TOWER_HNS)
Levels: 43–45. Tier T3. New ID; fixes the double-use of the Mahogany ID.
Gimmick: Boomburst volume; Choice Scarf Primeape.
Threat: Gyarados Dragon Dance (the one setup mon).
Counterplay: Electric, Rock, Ice, Fairy.
Hard-cap check: winnable at Lv 41–43 with an Electric/Rock type.

## Chad Grindwell 2 — Radio Tower (TRAINER_CHAD_GRINDWELL_RADIO_TOWER_HNS)
Levels: 43–45. Tier T3. Machamp (No Guard Dynamic Punch) is the ace, listed last.
Threat: Fake Out + Intimidate, Choice Band Tauros.
Counterplay: Psychic, Flying, Fairy; Ghosts dodge Fighting moves (watch Knock Off, Crunch).
Hard-cap check: winnable at Lv 41–43 with Psychic/Flying.

## Colonel Buckshot 2 — Radio Tower (TRAINER_COLONEL_BUCKSHOT_RADIO_TOWER_HNS)
Levels: 43–45. Tier T3. Clawitzer ace.
Gimmick: cannon battery; Mega Launcher and Sniper.
Counterplay: Grass, Electric (watch Fire Blast/Ice Beam); Ground vs Magneton.
Hard-cap check: winnable at Lv 41–43.

## Warden Pell 2 — Radio Tower (TRAINER_WARDEN_PELL_RADIO_TOWER_HNS)
Levels: 44–46. Tier T3. Ursaring (Flame Orb Guts) ace.
Gimmick: bulky single types, Stealth Rock, Guts.
Counterplay: Fighting, Water, Grass; outspeed or wall Ursaring (Intimidate and burn do not stop Guts).
Hard-cap check: winnable at Lv 42–44.

## President Rex Gaudy 1 — Radio Tower (TRAINER_PRESIDENT_REX_GAUDY_RADIO_TOWER_HNS)
Levels: 44–46. Tier G1. Kingambit (Supreme Overlord) last.
Gimmick: gold and inevitability; Pay Day, Make It Rain.
Threat: Kingambit snowballs as allies faint; Swords Dance is the single setup move.
Counterplay: Fire/Ground vs Steels, Fighting vs Kingambit (4x), Water vs Donphan.
Hard-cap check: winnable at Lv 42–44 with a Fighting or Fire type.

## Warden Pell 3 — Route 27 Border Wall (TRAINER_WARDEN_PELL_BORDER_WALL_HNS)
Levels: 52–54. Tier T3 with Full Restore x2. Ursaluna last.
Gimmick: Guts, bulk, Roar. Ursaring is the single sweeper (Swords Dance).
Counterplay: Fighting, Water, Grass; Ghosts for the Normal types.
Hard-cap check: winnable at Lv 50–52.

# Kanto bosses (P2-05)

## Brad Grindwell 2 — Cerulean Cape (TRAINER_BRAD_GRINDWELL_CERULEAN_CAPE_HNS)
T4, Lv 62–64. Rapidash, Revavroom, Lycanroc-Midnight (the team's one Focus Sash), Arcanine, Electivire. Counterplay: Water/Ground/Rock; Intimidate-immune Fire mons are weak to Water. One Stealth Rock user, no setup sweeper.

## Grindwell Bros — Crypto Mine (TRAINER_GRINDWELL_BROS_CRYPTO_MINE_HNS)
T4 double, Lv 63–65, six mons in one entry named GRINDWELLS (trainer names max 10 chars). Electivire + Gallade lead (Follow Me + Swords Dance). Machamp dropped for the six-slot limit. Counterplay: spread moves, Psychic/Fairy/Flying, Intimidate. Swords Dance (Gallade) and Bulk Up (Annihilape) are in different brothers' halves; exception logged.

## Colonel Buckshot 3 — Big Game Ranch (TRAINER_COLONEL_BUCKSHOT_BIG_GAME_RANCH_HNS)
T4, Lv 65–67. Blastoise (Shell Smash), Clawitzer, Magnezone, Armarouge (Calm Mind), Revavroom, Octillery. No Mega. Counterplay: Grass/Electric for the Waters, Ground/Water for Armarouge and Magnezone.

## President Rex Gaudy 2 — Comeback Rally (TRAINER_PRESIDENT_REX_GAUDY_COMEBACK_RALLY_HNS)
G2, Lv 71–73, AI Smart Trainer / Prediction, Full Restore ×3. Tyranitar @ Tyranitarite last (the only Mega). Counterplay: Fighting (4× vs Tyranitar/Kingambit), Water, Ground. Sand chip punishes non-Rock/Ground/Steel teams. Dragon Dance on Tyranitar is the sole setup move besides none elsewhere.

D.O.G.E. Auditors 1–3 (Lv 60–63, IVs 24), Crypto Bros 1–2 (new trainers instead of S.S. Aqua reclass, Lv 40–42), Viridian Rally Goers 1–3 (Lv 64–66). Basic Trainer AI, 4 moves each, no EVs.
